Staff Help & User Manual

Standard operating procedures (SOP) and clinical system guidelines.

📖 Welcome to Sunrise Dental Clinic System

This distributed web application is designed to replace manual paper logs at Sunrise Dental Clinic, Colombo. It automates patient intake, double-booking prevention, automated sequential referencing, procedure billing, and reporting.

1. User Roles & Security

  • ADMIN: Unrestricted access to reports, master catalogs, and user administration.
  • RECEPTIONIST: Front-desk operations (patient booking, searching, invoicing).
  • DENTIST: Clinical schedule reviews and patient treatment execution.
  • All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard BCrypt hashing.

2. Booking an Appointment

  • Navigate to New Appointment.
  • Enter patient name, address, and mobile number.
  • Select the appropriate Dentist and Treatment procedure.
  • Pick a date (future/today) and time between 08:00 and 17:00.
  • The system generates a unique reference (e.g. SDC-2026-0001).

3. Searching & Managing Appointments

  • Navigate to Search & Manage.
  • Enter the appointment reference (e.g., SDC-2026-0001).
  • View patient, doctor, and treatment information.
  • You can quickly jump to billing or cancel scheduled visits.

4. Invoicing & Printable Receipts

  • Navigate to Billing & Receipts.
  • Retrieve the appointment and enter any promotional discount (0-100%).
  • Formula: $\text{Total} = \text{Dentist Fee} + \text{Treatment Cost} - \text{Discount}$.
  • Click Issue Official Invoice.
  • Click Print Patient Receipt for a clean physical or PDF receipt.
